AFOSI PROJECT TEMPLATE
1. Project title
Unheard to Influential 
2.  What This Project Is About
Unheard to Influential is a 12-month initiative by Action for Sustainability Initiative (AFOSI) that harnesses Artificial Intelligence and digital advocacy to give voice to some of Kenya's most systematically excluded young people (young women living with HIV and young women with disabilities) residing in Mukuru, one of Nairobi's largest informal settlements.
The project bridges a critical gap: marginalized youth possess powerful first-hand knowledge of the policy failures that shape their lives, yet traditional advocacy channels have consistently shut them out. By equipping these young people with AI-driven storytelling tools, data analytics, and structured policy engagement pathways, Unheard to Influential transforms them from passive recipients of policy into active architects of change. The project will directly empower 150 young rights-holders and indirectly reach more than 500 community members delivering measurable advocacy wins at local, national, and regional levels.

3.  Why It Matters
In Nairobi's Mukuru informal settlement, young women living with HIV (PLHIV) and those with disabilities (PWDs) face a compounding set of barriers that strip them of both opportunity and voice. Stigma and discrimination drive them away from healthcare facilities and community forums. Economic hardship limits mobility and access to digital tools. Social norms, especially around gender and disability, further silence them in the spaces where decisions about their futures are made.
The consequences are tangible: service gaps go unaddressed, discriminatory practices continue unchallenged, and policies that could transform lives are shaped without input from those most affected. Existing advocacy platforms, designed largely for educated, connected, and able-bodied youth, fail to reach this population.
What makes this project unique is its dual approach: AI-powered technology that meets youth where they are, combined with rigorous policy engagement that carries their voices to where decisions are made. This is not awareness-raising, it is systemic change, driven by data and led by the young people who understand the system's failures most intimately.
4.  What We Do
Deploy AI-driven data collection tools and digital storytelling platforms that enable marginalized youth to document their experiences and generate real-time evidence of discrimination and service gaps.
Train 150 young PLHIV and PWD advocates in AI-powered policy engagement, digital literacy, leadership, and strategic communication equipping them to enter and influence decision-making spaces with confidence.
Facilitate direct, structured dialogues between empowered youth advocates and policymakers at national, regional, and continental levels, ensuring advocacy is grounded in evidence and directed at decision-makers.
Produce and submit policy briefs and concrete reform recommendations to health, SRHR, and inclusion-focused government bodies and advocacy networks.
5.  Key Solutions and Programs
Uses AI and digital tools to identify discrimination patterns, track policy implementation, and generate real-time data for advocacy.
Provides AI storytelling platforms, chatbots and virtual mentorship to build youth leadership and advocacy skills.
Combines technology and grassroots mobilization so marginalized youth can shape policies that affect their lives.

6.  Who It Serves

The project's primary beneficiaries are young women aged 18 to 35 who are living with HIV or who have a disability and reside in Mukuru, Nairobi. These individuals are systematically excluded from existing advocacy frameworks due to stigma, mobility limitations, digital inaccessibility, and economic hardship. They constitute the project's 150 direct rights-holders.
Beyond direct participants, the project reaches over 500 community members through public forums, radio programming, awareness campaigns, and policy dialogues amplifying its impact across Mukuru and into Nairobi's broader civic ecosystem.
7.  Impact

The project envisions a society in which policies, partnership/cooperation plans, and decision-making processes are the result of enhanced youth engagement, empowerment, and connection; and of improved intergenerational partnerships and action at local, national, continental, and cross-continental levels.

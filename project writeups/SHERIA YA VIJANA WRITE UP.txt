AFOSI PROJECT TEMPLATE
1. Project Title
Sheria Ya Vijana
Empower, Engage and Connect young people to lead Kenya's twin green and digital transition.

2. What This Project Is About
The project empowers youth in Nairobi and Kwale by strengthening their skills, leadership and participation in the green and digital economy. It builds youth capacities through training, mentorship, digital tools and policy engagement platforms. The overall goal is to enable youth and youth-led organisations to influence policy, access opportunities and drive sustainable socio-economic change.

3. Why It Matters
Kenyan youth face high unemployment, limited participation in policy processes and inadequate access to digital and green economy opportunities. Addressing these gaps is critical as Kenya transitions toward sustainable and digital development. The project provides the skills, spaces, and platforms youth need to participate meaningfully, advocate for inclusion, and benefit from emerging economic sectors.

4.What We Do
* Build youth skills in leadership, digital literacy, sustainability and green/digital entrepreneurship. 
* Provide mentorship, incubation and apprenticeships through digital and physical hubs.
* Develop youth-led platforms such as Kiongozi for AI-powered training, accountability and policy engagement.
* Establish and support youth forums, policy labs and national simulations for evidence-based advocacy.
* Strengthen youth-led organisations through capacity building and Financial Support to Third Parties (FSTP).

5. Key Solutions and Programs
Kiongozi Digital Platform (AFOSI)
* An AI-powered platform co-created with youth to deliver personalised digital training, leadership modules, and policy tools.
* Offers features for fund tracking, youth input on policy issues and digital learning on green and digital transitions. 
* Green & Digital Apprenticeship Hubs
* Provide hands-on mentorship, incubation, internship linkages, and AI-driven matching between youth and mentors.
* Target youth-led enterprises and YLOs operating in green and digital sectors. 
* FSTP Youth-Led Enterprise Grants
* Financial and technical support for youth-led organisations to scale innovative green and digital ventures.  
.
6. Who It Serves
* 5,875 young people aged 15-29 (including women and persons with disabilities).
* Youth-led organisations, community groups, social enterprises, influencers, and youth workers.
* Final beneficiaries include 1.8 million youth in Nairobi and Kwale who benefit from awareness, policy influence, and strengthened systems
7. Impact
* Youth have increased knowledge, leadership capacity, and economic empowerment in the green and digital economy.
* Youth and youth-led organisations actively participate in policy design and decision-making at community, county, and national levels.
* Increased number of youth-led enterprises adopting green/digital innovations and accessing funding, mentorship, and networks.
